# Caption Generator Pro - After Effects Extension Panel

**Caption Generator Pro (AE)** is an AI-powered extension panel for **Adobe After Effects**. It automates video captioning, speech transcription, and animated text layer creation directly inside your active composition with a single click.

---

## 🌟 Key Features for After Effects

- **Active Comp Audio Extractor**: Automatically scans active composition layers for speech audio and generates wave data.
- **Kinetic Pop-In Animation**: Automatically creates animated text layers with scale keyframes (`0% → 115% → 100%`) for popular Hormozi-style short-form video captions.
- **Shared Speech Models**: Shares downloaded Whisper models (`base.pt`, `medium.pt`, etc.) with the Premiere Pro extension via `~/.cache/whisper`. No duplicate downloads needed!
- **In-Panel Subtitle Cue Editor**: View subtitle cues, edit text inline, add/delete cues, and click any timecode to jump the After Effects playhead instantly to that exact frame.
- **Custom Dark UI System**: Identical sleek Adobe dark theme aesthetic matching the Premiere Pro extension.

---

## 🚀 1-Click Installation

1. Double-click **`install_plugin.bat`** inside the `After Effects` folder.
2. Launch **Adobe After Effects**.
3. Open **Window -> Extensions -> Caption Generator Pro (AE)**.
